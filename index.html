I'm Harris Waseem — a finance and data analyst with 4+ years of active trading experience focused on options and technical analysis. My academic and personal work spans DCF modeling, M&A evaluation, and market strategy, bridging quantitative rigor with real trading intuition.

My projects draw on Bloomberg terminal data, multi-year company financials, and original WACC/FCF frameworks — applied to real companies like Stryker Corporation, and real markets I've traded actively.

Alongside financial modeling, I co-founded FINDEX, a SaaS-based financial article aggregation engine designed to democratize access to institutional-quality market data.
